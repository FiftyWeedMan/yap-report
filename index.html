🔒 your chat never leaves your device

  • no server. everything runs in your browser. there's nowhere to upload to.
  • locked down. the page is blocked from sending data anywhere. not even analytics.
  • works offline. don't trust us? load this page, turn off your Wi-Fi, then drop your chat.
  • nothing saved. close the tab and it's gone. no cookies, no storage.
  • numbers hidden. unsaved contacts get masked, and you can swap every name for "Person A, B…" before sharing.
  • open source. read the code yourself.

how to export your chat

  1. open the group in WhatsApp
  2. tap ⋮ → More → Export chat
  3. choose Without media
  4. save it to Files / Drive, or send it to yourself, then drop it above
